Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15/11/2025

FounderGram (the “App”) is developed and published by FounderGram. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information that is necessary for the App to function.

1.1 Account Information

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely using Supabase Authentication — we cannot see it)

1.2 User-Generated Content

You may create and upload content, such as:

  • Posts
  • Profile details (bio, interests, account type)
  • Images used in posts
  • Messages sent through the chat feature

This content is stored securely in Supabase and used only inside the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data only to provide the main features of the App, such as:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Displaying posts and content to other users
  • Enabling real-time chat
  • Personalizing your profile
  • Allowing you to follow and interact with other users

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or third parties.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted backend service. Supabase stores user information, posts, and messages securely using modern encryption and security practices.

We never store passwords in plain text.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not:

  • Share your data with third-party companies
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Use your data for advertising

Your data is used only inside the App for its core functionality.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data through:

  • Supabase secure authentication
  • Encrypted communication (HTTPS)
  • Access control and permission rules

6. Your Choices

You may:

  • Delete your posts
  • Update your profile information
  • Request account deletion by contacting us

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update the App or start collecting additional data, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, contact us at:

Email: foundergramteam@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to FounderGram as released in the MVP on app stores. It does not create any contractual rights beyond those required by applicable law.
