Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15/11/2025

FounderGram (the “App”) is developed and published by FounderGram. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. No Personal Data Collection

For this MVP release we do not collect, store, or process any personal information from users through the App. The App does not request, save, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal details (name, address, phone number)
  • Financial information
  • Contact lists or address book data
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Device identifiers (beyond standard platform identifiers required for app functioning)
  • Photos, media, or files from your device
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

2. No Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect user data, we do not share any user information with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

3. No Cookies or Tracking

The App does not use cookies, trackers, or any form of analytics that records user behaviour. If we add analytics in the future we will update this policy and inform users.

4. Supabase and App Functionality

The App uses Supabase for authentication, storage, and realtime features. Supabase may store records necessary for app functionality (for example user accounts and posts) — these are not collected by us outside the App and are controlled in your Supabase project.

If in future releases the App starts to collect personal data (for example profile information, images uploaded by users, chat messages, etc.), we will update this Privacy Policy and clearly explain what is collected and how it is used.

5. Security

Even though we do not collect personal data in this MVP, we take reasonable steps to keep the App and any backend services secure. Supabase connections should be secured using the recommended keys and environment variables. Do not embed service_role keys in client apps.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if we change how the App handles data. When we make changes we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If we begin collecting personal data in the future we will inform users via the App and update this policy prior to data collection.
